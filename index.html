The Basics

What Exactly Are Pure THC Drops?

They are liquid concentrates, usually THC suspended in a neutral carrier oil such as MCT, packed in a dropper bottle. The dropper lets you control each serving far more precisely than most edibles can.

Because the formula is concentrated, small changes matter. That is why we teach every new customer to read the label before reaching for the bottle.

How to Read a Bottle Like a Pro

Five details on any label tell you most of what you need to know.

  1. Batch number: matches the lab report for that exact production run.
  2. Total potency: the full amount in the bottle, not per dose.
  3. Serving size: divide total potency by servings for real strength.
  4. Ingredients: check the carrier oil if you have allergies.
  5. Expiry date: older oil loses quality, so always check.

Responsible Use Matters

THC affects everyone differently. A few habits keep the experience comfortable and safe for you and the people around you.

  • Wait a full two hours before taking more
  • Never drive or operate machinery after use
  • Store bottles locked away from kids and pets
  • Check with your doctor about medication interactions
  • Keep products in their original labeled container

Frequently Asked Questions

How do pure THC drops differ from CBD oil?

CBD oil is non-intoxicating, while THC drops are noticeably stronger and can affect how you feel and think. Start small and ask us for guidance.

How many drops should I take?

That depends on bottle strength and your experience. We help you work out a small starting serving and explain how to adjust.

Do I need a medical card?

Product rules vary by type. Call us and we will explain what applies, and we can point you to a local physician by appointment.

Can I see the lab report first?

Of course. Ask at the counter and we will show you the batch report for your bottle.

How should I store my drops?

Keep the bottle sealed, cool and dark, and well out of reach of children and pets.
